ROUND 1 · CREATIVE DIRECTOR D1

Money on paper,
read in daylight.

Light-first. Three hues. No gradients. No red anywhere in the product — over the line is a texture, not a colour. Exactly one number per screen is allowed to be large, and it is set in a serif.

Working name recommendation: Spare · runner-up: Leeway

4 · The mark — three concepts

Tests each must pass: legible at 16px in one colour; not a fruit, coin, wallet, pig, chart, arrow or shield; carries room rather than growth; two elements or fewer. Shown at 48px in clay and at 16px in ink. The geometry is fixed and is never bound to data.

M1 · The Open Ring
The month, unclosed. A fixed 62° gap on the upper-right diagonal is the room you have left. The gap never moves and is never bound to a value — the moment it does, it is a progress ring, which is a breakable mechanic and is banned.
M2 · The Basin  — recommended
Where the month lands. One open bowl, one resting dot, sat right of centre so it reads as settled, with room either side rather than balanced. A catch, a cupped hand, a valley floor. The bowl always opens upward.
M3 · The Rule and the Dot
The line is the budget, the dot is where you are, the gap between them is the product. Strongest as the recurring UI divider (below) rather than as a standalone icon — which is the point: the mark is a compression of something on every screen.

6 · The bar, re-materialised

Magnitude still lives in the number — the grammar is untouched. What changes is the material. Over the line is the same colour in a hatched fill: a different kind of thing, not a hotter one. This needs ratification against AGENTS.md non-negotiable 2, which currently specifies red.

8 · The rules, in short

  • Light is the default. Dark ships, is designed separately, and is never a token-for-token remap.
  • Three hues exist: paper, ink, clay. A fourth is a bug.
  • No red anywhere in the product — including errors and destructive actions.
  • Colour never encodes valence. Under and over are the same colour.
  • Max two clay elements per screen. Countable in a screenshot.
  • Categories are never colour-coded — no swatch, dot, tint or pictogram on a user’s own word.
  • Zero gradients in src/, and exactly one repeating-linear-gradient: the hatch.
  • Exactly one element per screen is ≥40px, and the serif is used only at ≥40px.
  • Weights 400 / 500 / 600 only. No all-caps letterspaced text, anywhere.
  • Every money figure sets tabular-nums; mono survives only inside raw statement provenance.
  • Content column capped at 520px / 62 characters on every viewport.
  • Icons: 24px grid, 1.75px stroke, no fills, 18 max — a nineteenth means deleting one.
  • Two radii (4 / 14 / 999) and one shadow token. Nothing else casts a shadow.
  • The mark’s geometry is never data-bound. No part of the logo animates to show a value.
  • Three visual signatures — the one serif figure, no red, paper. A fourth retires one.
