05 · motion

"Is this it?" — mostly, yes

Three signature moments was your own instruction taken literally: a few signature pieces, not a busy design. But the demo only showed those three and none of the invisible layer — the transitions you would actually feel all day. So the question is not which animations, it is how much.

A · RestrainedWhat was specced. What you saw.
  • The three signature moments
  • Screen changes cross-fade
  • Bars settle only on the add
  • Nothing else moves
B · Present — suggestedThe invisible layer made deliberate. Still three signatures.
  • Everything in A, plus:
  • Every bar settles when its value changes
  • Sheets rise and fall with real weight
  • Collapsed rows open with a short stagger
  • The nav pill's indicator slides between tabs
  • Figures cross-fade on month change
C · AliveB plus character. The risk is the busyness you said you did not want.
  • Everything in B, plus:
  • App open plays a short entry sequence
  • Headers respond to scroll
  • Pull-to-refresh has its own gesture
  • Marks and icons animate on tap
noteOnly A has been built and felt. B and C are descriptions — if you pick one, the next thing I build is that level running on your real home screen, not another spec.
