Sampled from Arrakis

The images came through. Rather than eyeball them I pulled the actual pixel values out of all three — the eyes, the skin, the sky, the sand. The numbers say I had it backwards.

what I was missing

The blue is soft. I kept making it loud.

Every blue in your references sits between 34% and 47% saturation. Mine was at 92%. That is not a near miss — it is the wrong idea about where the glow comes from.

the actual ruleThe eyes glow because they sit in deep warm shadow, not because the pigment is strong. Chani's skin samples at #6B5231 — a dark umber, not the pale bronze I built. I kept turning the blue up when I should have been turning the ground down. Contrast, not chroma.
one exceptionThe only genuinely saturated thing in any of your references is the poster's hot orange at 90%. Which is exactly where the warning colour should live — so clay finally has a number, and it is a loud one.
