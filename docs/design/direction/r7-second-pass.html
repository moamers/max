The Eyes of Ibad

"Boring" was diagnosable. Everything in the last set sat in the same register — same value, same saturation, nothing lit. A muted ground and a muted accent leaves no energy anywhere.

The fix is in your own reference. The most striking thing in Dune is a luminous blue against desert-warm skin — high chroma against low chroma. All three below do that; they disagree about how bright the desert is.

01 · palette

One thing is allowed to glow

Night is kept as the family — you picked it — but pushed until it does something. Roles unchanged: blue acts, green settles, clay warns. What changes is that the blue is now the brightest object in the frame instead of another dusty tone.

what changedThree levers, all missing last time. Saturation contrast — the ground drops in chroma so the blue can rise. Value contrast — a wider gap between the lightest and darkest thing on screen. One hot note — clay is now genuinely hot, because a warning that whispers is not a warning.

02 · the oasis

It needed water, not another arc

You were right — two nested arcs with a dot above is a wifi symbol, and a baby. The problem was that the state had no water in it. Every version below adds an ellipse, and the ellipse is what makes it read as a pool rather than a signal.

how it still morphsThe skeleton grows by one element: two strokes, a sun, and a pool. In dune and horizon the pool's height collapses to nothing and it lies flat on the ground line, so it never has to appear or disappear — it opens and closes. Dune stays the fixed mark, as you picked.
