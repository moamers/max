Round 10 · warmer

Somewhere Warm

I sampled the wrong thing. Those stills are Dune the film — deliberately desaturated, grim, at war. You want dune the place: a holiday. Marrakech and Palm Springs, not Arrakis.

That is why every pass has felt toned out. I was faithfully reproducing a colour grade designed to look bleak.

01 · palette

Pool blue, not eye blue

Same desert, different day. Sun on white plaster, tiled water, terracotta in full light, palm green. Saturation goes up, not down — and the ground gets light again, because holidays are bright.

what carries overOnly the structure. Blue acts, green settles, hot coral warns — and the warning is still the most saturated thing on the screen, which is the one lesson the film references did teach.

02 · the mark

Fly through it, don't bend it

Your idea, built. It isn't a morph any more — it's one landscape with depth, and changing scope moves the camera through it. The dune really is sitting small in the distance behind the oasis the whole time. Press Month and you fly to it.

why this is betterA morph says two shapes are the same thing bending. A dolly says they are different things at different distances — which is exactly what a week, a month and a year are. Pulling back through time becomes literal rather than a metaphor.
