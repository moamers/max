# The settled design direction

Eleven rounds, three directors, and a lot of founder correction. **This is what
was decided.** Everything else in this folder is working papers; where they
disagree with this file, this file wins.

Live: `r11-the-look-settled.html`.

## Palette · Poolside

Whitewash and chlorine — a desert *holiday*, not Arrakis. The film references
were sampled and then deliberately abandoned: that colour grade is built to look
bleak, which is why five passes came back "toned out".

| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | `#FFFFFF` → `#ECF6F8` | `#0F262E` → `#061218` |
| Card | `#FFFFFF` | `#122F38` |
| Ink | `#14303A` | `#EAF6F8` |
| Ink 2 | `#4E6B74` | `#8FB2BB` |
| Rule / track | `#DCEAED` / `#E4EFF1` | `#1C424E` / `#17353F` |
| **Blue — acts** | `#0093B0` | `#22C8E8` |
| **Green — settles** | `#2E9179` | `#57CDA8` |
| **Coral — warns** | `#F0521F` | `#FF7A4D` |

**The roles are the rule.** Blue is anything you can touch. Green means done or
fine. Coral is attention only, **at most once per screen**, and it is the most
saturated thing on the page — that is what makes it read as a warning rather
than decoration.

## Type · Bookish

**Newsreader** (300/400) for the one large figure per screen. **Libre Franklin**
(400/600) for everything else. No monospace anywhere; `font-variant-numeric:
tabular-nums` does the aligning that a terminal face used to.

## Structure · A

**Home is the current week**, transactions collapsed. Month and Year are their
own tabs behind a floating pill. The per-week breakdown leaves home entirely and
lives on the Month tab as totals only.

- Old month opens on its **last** week; a future month on its **first**.
- Month tab is horizontal cards, **one-offs first** — adding one is the common act.

This was the founder's own proposal and it is better than either director
reached: home's nine figures go to one headline and three rows, not by hiding
anything but by making the rest another screen's job.

## The mark · oasis → dune → horizon

One mark at three distances, **morphing with the scope**. Skeleton: two cubics,
a sun, and a pool whose height collapses to nothing away from the oasis, so it
opens and closes rather than appearing.

- Oasis is **still water** — no palm. Two attempts at a palm were rejected.
- Dune keeps its **crossing ridges**.
- **Dune is the fixed mark** — app icon, favicon, anywhere outside the app.
- The morph is one of the three signature moments.

**The rule that keeps it honest:** the mark moves with *where you are*, never
with *a value*. Geometry that responds to spending is a progress ring, which is
a breakable mechanic and is banned.

## Motion · level C, alive

Three signatures — **the Tape** (any figure opens its evidence), **the Landing**
(the add commits), **the Mark** — over a functional layer that is visible rather
than invisible: bars settle whenever a value changes, sheets have weight,
collapsed rows stagger open, the pill's indicator slides.

Numerals never travel. A numeral is precise to its last digit, so every frame in
a count-up is a specific claim that is true of nothing. Bars may settle; figures
re-state.

## Bars

A flat **ink** fill on a quiet track. No colour ramp — with coral reserved for
warnings, a bar that heats up as it fills would be the one thing shouting on
every screen.

*Note: the founder chose "rotated hues" for the ramp back on the decision bench,
but that was against the old dark-and-lime palette. Poolside settles it by
implication and he has been told so.*

## Still open

**The name.** Nothing has survived — Max, Spare, Leeway and Margin were all
rejected. It wants its own pass now there is a look to hang it on.
